# Employee Policy FAQ

## How much paid time off (PTO) do I get?
Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per year, accrued monthly. Unused PTO up to 5 days carries over into the next calendar year. Submit requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for absences longer than 3 days.

## What is the remote work policy?
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from HR. Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10am to 3pm in their local time zone.

## How do I report a security incident?
Email security@example.com or use the #security-incident Slack channel immediately. Report lost devices, suspected phishing, or unauthorized access within 1 hour of discovery.
