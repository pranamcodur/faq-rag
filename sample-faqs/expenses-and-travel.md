# Expenses and Travel FAQ

## How do I submit an expense claim?
Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over $25. Claims without receipts are rejected.

## How long does reimbursement take?
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, usually within 10 business days. You can track the status of a claim in the finance portal.

## How do I book business travel?
Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool. Trips estimated at more than $1,500 need manager approval before booking. Flights under 6 hours are economy class.

## What is the meal allowance when travelling?
The daily meal allowance is $60 for domestic trips and $80 for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
