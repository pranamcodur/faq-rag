# New Hire Onboarding FAQ

## What happens on my first day?
Collect your badge from reception at 9:30am. IT hands out your laptop, and the HR orientation session starts at 10:30am. You will meet your manager and your onboarding buddy before lunch.

## Which accounts will I get?
Your email, Slack, HR portal and finance portal accounts are created before your start date. You receive a temporary password by text message and must change it at first login. Set up two-factor authentication within your first 7 days.

## What is the onboarding buddy programme?
Every new hire is paired with an experienced colleague for their first 8 weeks. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps you find the right people and documents.

## How long is the probation period?
Probation lasts 3 months. You have a check-in with your manager at the end of month 1 and a formal review at the end of month 3.
