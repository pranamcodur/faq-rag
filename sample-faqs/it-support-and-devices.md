# IT Support and Devices FAQ

## How do I get help from IT?
Open a ticket in the IT helpdesk portal or email helpdesk@example.com. For urgent issues during business hours, post in the #it-help Slack channel. The target first response time is 4 business hours.

## What should I do if my laptop is lost or stolen?
Tell the IT helpdesk and security@example.com immediately so the laptop can be locked and remotely wiped. Also report it as a security incident within 1 hour of discovering the loss.

## How often are laptops replaced?
Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or sooner if they are damaged beyond repair. Request a replacement through the IT helpdesk portal.

## Can I use my personal phone or laptop for work?
Personal phones can access email and Slack if they have a screen lock and the company device management profile installed. Personal laptops cannot be used to access internal systems.

## When do I need the VPN?
Use the VPN whenever you work on public or shared networks such as cafes, airports or hotels. Install the client from the software portal and sign in with your company credentials and your two-factor authentication code.
