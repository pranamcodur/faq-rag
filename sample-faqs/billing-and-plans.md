# Billing and Plans FAQ

## Which plans are available?
There are three plans: Starter at $12 per user per month, Business at $29 per user per month, and Enterprise with custom pricing. SSO and audit logs are included on Business and Enterprise only.

## How do I change my plan?
An admin can go to Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Where do I find my invoices?
Open Settings > Billing > Invoices. Only admins and billing contacts can see them. You can change the billing email address on the same page.

## Can I get a refund?
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase or renewal. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the period ends.

## Which payment methods are accepted?
We accept major credit and debit cards on all plans. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms.
