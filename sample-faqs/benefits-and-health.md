# Benefits and Health FAQ

## When does my health insurance start?
Coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. Dependants can be added during open enrolment each November, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Does the company contribute to retirement savings?
Yes. The company matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of your salary. Matched contributions are vested immediately, so they are yours from day one.

## How much parental leave do I get?
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid leave and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Is there a budget for learning and conferences?
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Get manager approval first, then claim the cost as an expense in the finance portal.
